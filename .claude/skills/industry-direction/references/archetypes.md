# Niche archetypes — starting points, not templates

Each entry is where a direction STARTS. Adapt every line to the real client:
their building, their materials, their customers. If the finished brief still
matches the archetype word for word, it is not finished.

Type suggestions list a free Google Fonts option first (usable on any
budget) and a licensed upgrade second (a per-client cost — the client needs
their own licence). Hex values are a starting palette; re-derive from the
client's premises, product or logo, and check every text/background pair
for WCAG AA (4.5:1 body, 3:1 large type).

---

## 1. Luxury fashion boutique
- **Type:** Bodoni Moda or Playfair Display (Didone contrast) for display, Jost for body. Licensed: Canela, Ogg.
- **Palette:** paper `#F6F4F0`, ink `#151413`, one fabric colour drawn from the stock (oxblood `#5A1B1F`, or sage, or camel), warm grey `#8C857D`.
- **Motion:** luxury tempo, 1.0–1.6s. Clip-path image reveals, slow crossfades. One thing moves at a time.
- **Imagery:** editorial, generous negative space, garments on figure and flat on seamless. Their own shoot beats any stock.
- **Signature moment:** a full-bleed lookbook Carousel3D, or a hero product that scales down into the grid on scroll.
- **Avoid:** cream + terracotta + serif (the saturated AI look), sale-banner energy, dense product grids above the fold.

## 2. Golf club / country club
- **Type:** Libre Caslon Display or Cormorant Garamond for display; Figtree or Hanken Grotesk for body and figures. Licensed: Tiempos, GT Sectra.
- **Palette:** fairway `#1E3A2A`, clubhouse cream `#F2ECDF`, brass `#A6864A`, ink `#161614`, heather `#6E6A7A`.
- **Motion:** heritage-hospitality, 0.8–1.1s, soft parallax on course photography.
- **Imagery:** first light on the course, dew, the clubhouse interior, real members — not models in branded polos.
- **Signature moment:** the course as a scorecard — StackCards hole by hole with par, yardage and stroke index as the typographic system.
- **Avoid:** sports-club royal blue, stock golfers, crest clipart, anything that reads "municipal leisure centre".

## 3. Prestige estate agent
- **Type:** Instrument Serif or Libre Caslon for display; Schibsted Grotesk or Hanken Grotesk with tabular figures for prices and specs. Licensed: Domaine + Söhne.
- **Palette:** stone `#EDE9E3`, ink navy `#15202B`, forest `#2B3A2F`, brass accent `#9C7C45`.
- **Motion:** premium, 0.6–0.9s. Property imagery leads; type never competes with it.
- **Imagery:** architectural, golden hour, interiors shot level and wide.
- **Signature moment:** a property Carousel3D where price, beds and baths form a strict typographic line; floorplan line-work that draws on scroll.
- **Avoid:** red-and-yellow volume-agent energy; a search form as the hero.

## 4. Local / volume estate agent
- **Type:** a clear grotesk for everything — Hanken Grotesk, Figtree or Schibsted. No display serif.
- **Palette:** from the existing brand colour, used confidently, plus neutrals.
- **Motion:** quick, 0.3–0.5s. Search and valuation CTAs are never delayed.
- **Signature moment:** "valued in [their town] this month" as a real, live strip — only with real data.
- **Avoid:** borrowing prestige-agent slowness; it reads as pretension to this buyer.

## 5. Fine-dining restaurant
- **Type:** Gloock, Young Serif or EB Garamond italic for display; a restrained sans (Jost, Hanken) for info. Licensed: GT Super, Ogg.
- **Palette:** from the room itself — plaster `#E8E1D6`, charcoal `#1B1A19`, a wine or ember tone.
- **Motion:** slow, 1.0–1.4s. Dark photography, light type.
- **Imagery:** hands, fire, plates shot close and dark. Never stock food.
- **Signature moment:** the menu as a typographic set piece; ScrollText for the chef's statement.
- **Avoid:** carousels of twenty dishes, booking widget as the design.

## 6. Café, bakery, casual food
- **Type:** Fraunces (soft, SOFT/WONK axes) or Bricolage Grotesque for display; Figtree body.
- **Palette:** from the produce — crust, butter, berry, oat. Warm and saturated, not beige.
- **Motion:** warm and quick, 0.4–0.6s.
- **Signature moment:** a VelocityMarquee of today's bakes; a handwritten-feeling but real typeface, never a chalkboard font.
- **Avoid:** chalkboard fonts, stock latte art.

## 7. Hotel, spa, retreat
- **Type:** Marcellus or Cormorant (light weights) for display; Jost body. Licensed: Canela Light.
- **Palette:** linen `#F1EDE6`, stone `#B9B1A6`, water or forest tone, deep ink.
- **Motion:** slowest of all, 1.2–1.6s, gentle parallax, long crossfades.
- **Signature moment:** a scroll-driven room or grounds tour.
- **Avoid:** a booking engine dominating the page; stock "woman in robe" imagery.

## 8. Law firm
- **Type:** Source Serif 4 or Newsreader for headings; IBM Plex Sans for body. Licensed: Tiempos + Söhne.
- **Palette:** ink navy `#131C27`, parchment `#F4F1EA`, a single muted accent (bottle green or claret).
- **Motion:** premium-service, near invisible, 0.5–0.7s.
- **Signature moment:** ScrollText for the firm's principles; practice areas as StackCards.
- **Avoid:** gavels, scales of justice, marquees, anything playful or velocity-driven.

## 9. Accountancy, finance, wealth management
- **Type:** IBM Plex Sans or Schibsted Grotesk with tabular numerals; optional Source Serif accent.
- **Palette:** cool — slate `#1D2530`, paper white, one confident accent.
- **Motion:** precise, 0.5–0.7s. Count-ups only for real, sourced figures.
- **Avoid:** stock handshakes, invented statistics, "growth arrow" graphics.

## 10. Dental or aesthetic clinic
- **Type:** Manrope or Outfit body with a soft serif accent (Fraunces at low contrast).
- **Palette:** warm neutrals with one calm tone — not clinical blue-white.
- **Motion:** calm, 0.6–0.8s.
- **Signature moment:** treatments as StackCards with transparent pricing.
- **Avoid:** stock smiles; before/after images without written consent.

## 11. Gym, fitness, sport
- **Type:** Anton, Bebas Neue or Barlow Condensed for display; a sturdy grotesk body.
- **Palette:** high contrast plus one energetic colour drawn from their kit or space — not default neon on black.
- **Motion:** snappy, 0.25–0.5s, velocity-reactive, video-led.
- **Signature moment:** VelocityMarquee; the class timetable as a bold typographic grid.
- **Avoid:** generic neon-on-black; slow luxury tempo.

## 12. Construction and trades
- **Type:** Archivo or Barlow — sturdy, legible grotesk.
- **Palette:** from materials — slate, timber, concrete — with the brand colour. Safety orange is the cliché.
- **Motion:** quick and minimal. The phone number is visible at all times and no animation ever delays it.
- **Signature moment:** project gallery with before/after; accreditation strip.
- **Avoid:** anything that makes a customer with a burst pipe wait.

## 13. Prestige automotive
- **Type:** an extended grotesk — Archivo on its width axis, Unbounded, or Syne. Licensed: a wide Neue Haas cut.
- **Palette:** dark metallic neutrals, one heritage colour.
- **Motion:** cinematic, slow, scroll-scrubbed turntable or reveal.
- **Signature moment:** a model Carousel3D; a scroll-driven car turntable.
- **Avoid:** chrome gradients on everything.

## 14. Jeweller or watchmaker
- **Type:** Didone (Bodoni Moda, Libre Bodoni) or a fine serif; light sans for detail.
- **Palette:** black velvet or white silk — one, not both — with metal tones.
- **Motion:** ultra slow. Tilt with glare, so the product literally catches the light.
- **Imagery:** macro product photography.
- **Avoid:** sparkle effects, lens-flare stock.

## 15. Wedding or event venue
- **Type:** a romantic serif (Cormorant Garamond) with a script accent used for one word at most; never script body text.
- **Palette:** from the grounds through the seasons.
- **Motion:** soft, 0.9–1.2s.
- **Signature moment:** a seasonal gallery; ScrollText for the venue's story.
- **Avoid:** pastel overload, script everywhere.

## 16. Architecture or interiors practice
- **Type:** a strict Swiss grotesk (Hanken Grotesk, Schibsted). Licensed: Neue Haas Grotesk, Söhne.
- **Palette:** monochrome plus real material swatches from their projects.
- **Motion:** precise, 0.5–0.7s. Structure is the aesthetic.
- **Signature moment:** a project index where the image follows the cursor.
- **Avoid:** rounded cards, decorative motion — this buyer reads ornament as a lack of rigour.
