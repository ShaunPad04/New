# Design Direction — Watch Company Concept

## Status
Initial direction only. This is not an approved final visual system.

## Visual intent
- Premium horology/editorial feel rather than a generic ecommerce template
- Dark, controlled presentation with disciplined contrast
- Macro product imagery and cinematic motion used as focal moments
- Typography should feel precise, modern and premium; avoid novelty luxury clichés
- Layout should use strong negative space, deliberate scale changes and restrained UI chrome
- Interactions should support the product story, not compete with it

## Motion
Use motion sparingly and intentionally:
- slow macro camera movement
- controlled scroll-linked reveals where justified
- subtle depth/parallax on product media
- no constant decorative motion
- provide a coherent reduced-motion experience

## Imagery
Prioritise:
- real client imagery when available
- high-quality macro dial/case/bracelet/movement shots
- product video suitable for hero/background use
- consistent lighting, colour grade and material rendering

Do not present AI-generated imagery as factual product photography without the user's approval.

## Quality baseline
Before presentation or launch, verify responsive behaviour, keyboard access, contrast, image sizing, LCP/CLS, metadata, noindex state for concept builds, and production build integrity.
