## Before you choose [06]
## Questions, answered.
Speak with the team ↗
1. What does "gold filled" mean? — A gold filled piece has a thick layer of real gold bonded to a base metal, so it looks and wears like solid gold for far less. S&L Jewellers stock both and will always tell you which one you are looking at. They are not affiliated with the brands whose styles they sell.
2. Do you buy gold? — Yes. S&L Jewellers buy all gold and jewellery in any condition, including broken pieces and odd earrings. Bring it into the shop for a price, or send photographs and the weight first.
3. How do I know what I am buying? — Ask the shop. They will tell you the metal, the weight, the size and whether a piece is solid or gold filled before you buy.
4. Can I see a piece before buying? — Yes. Come to 49 Cambridge Street, Cleethorpes DN35 8HD. Message ahead and they will put it aside and confirm it is still in.
5. Do you deliver? — Next day delivery is available. Confirm postage and timing with the shop when you enquire.
6. Can I return something? — Ask the shop before you buy. Returns are agreed piece by piece over the counter, so the team will tell you where you stand on anything you are looking at.
7. Can I part-exchange? — Yes. Put the value of gold you already own towards something else in the shop, and the team will work out the difference.
8. Are the prices and availability up to date? — Catalogue prices and availability are a September 2026 snapshot. Confirm current price and availability with the shop before purchasing.
