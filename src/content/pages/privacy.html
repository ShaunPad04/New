Who we are

Zen Den Beauty and Wellbeing Centre, 51 Fieldhouse Road, Humberston, Grimsby DN36 4UJ, is the data controller for the personal information described here. That means we decide why and how it is used.

This notice covers this website only. Booking an appointment happens on Fresha, which handles your information under its own privacy policy — see “Booking through Fresha” below.

What we collect, and why

We collect as little as possible. There is no account to create, no newsletter sign-up, and nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

  • The enquiry form — your name, email address, message, and your telephone number if you choose to give it. We use these to read and reply to your enquiry, and for nothing else.
  • Website analytics — anonymised statistics about which pages are visited, but only if you agree. See the cookie notice.
  • Server logs — our hosting provider records the IP address and browser of every visit, as all web servers do, to deliver pages and to defend against attacks.

Our lawful basis

For the enquiry form we rely on legitimate interests: you have contacted us about our services and we have a plain interest in replying. You can object to this at any time, though we would then be unable to answer you.

For analytics we rely on your consent, which you give or refuse in the cookie banner and can change whenever you like.

For server logs we rely on legitimate interests in keeping the site available and secure.

The enquiry form

When the form is submitted, its contents are emailed to the salon. They are not stored in a database on this website.

The form carries a hidden field that real visitors never see. Automated spam tends to fill it in, and anything that does is discarded. Nothing is recorded about you for this check.

Services we rely on

Running the site means a small number of other companies process information on our behalf or receive it because your browser contacts them directly.

  • Our hosting provider — serves the pages and keeps the security logs described above.
  • Google Maps — the map on our contact and home pages is loaded from Google. Your browser contacts Google to fetch it, so Google receives your IP address and may set cookies, whatever you chose in our cookie banner. If you would rather that did not happen, avoid the pages carrying the map, or block third-party cookies in your browser.
  • Fresha — several photographs of the salon are served from Fresha, because that is where they are stored. Loading those pages means your browser contacts Fresha and Fresha receives your IP address.
  • Google Analytics — statistics only, only with your consent, and never enabled by default.

Booking through Fresha

Every “book” button on this site sends you to Fresha, our booking system. Once you are there you are dealing with Fresha, not with this website, and Fresha is a separate data controller for anything you enter — your contact details, your appointment history, and your payment information.

Read Fresha's own privacy policy for what it does with that information. Nothing you type into Fresha passes through this website.

How long we keep it

Enquiry emails are kept only as long as we need them to deal with the enquiry and any follow-up, and are then deleted.

Analytics statistics are aggregated and hold nothing that identifies you. Server logs are held briefly by our hosting provider under its own schedule.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to do any of the following, free of charge, and we will respond within one month.

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you, and give you a copy.
  • Correct anything that is wrong.
  • Delete it, where we have no continuing reason to keep it.
  • Restrict how we use it, or object to our using it.
  • Provide it in a portable format.
  • Withdraw your consent to analytics, at any time and without giving a reason.

Questions and complaints

Please speak to us first — ring the salon on 07873 220636, or write to us at 51 Fieldhouse Road, Humberston, Grimsby DN36 4UJ. We would far rather put something right ourselves.

If you are not satisfied, you have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK's data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this notice

If we change how the site handles personal information, we will change this page and update the date at the top of it.
