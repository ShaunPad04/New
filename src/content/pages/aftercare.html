Before,between, after.

The practical bits worth knowing — the patch test that has to happen first, how to look after lashes between infills, and which treatments are designed to run as a course. All of it comes from Zen Den’s own booking notes.

Patch tests

Tinting, perming and lifting treatments need a patch test before your first appointment. It takes fifteen minutes, it's free, and without it the treatment cannot go ahead.

24 hours before

A patch test is required at least 24 hours before any lash tint, brow tint, lash lift, LVL or HD Brows appointment. Without it your appointment cannot proceed and you may still be charged for the booked time.

48 hours before HD Brow Sculpt

HD Brow Sculpt needs its patch test at least 48 hours ahead rather than 24.

Booking the test

Patch Test — Tint, Lift & Sculpt Treatments is a free 15-minute appointment on the booking menu. Book it alongside your first treatment.

Source: Zen Den service notes, Fresha

Lash extensions

Nouveau extensions last longest when they're looked after between appointments — and infills are only possible if enough of the set is still there.

Arrive clean

For the best results, arrive with clean, make-up-free lashes and eyes.

The 50% rule

An infill refreshes a set applied by us, and needs over 50% of the extensions still in place to qualify. If less than half remain, the full-set price applies.

Between sets

A Lash Removal, Deep Cleanse and Conditioning treatment removes build-up and keeps extensions comfortable. Available for lashes applied by us.

Removals

Removal is free for lashes applied by us. Must be 16 and over.

Source: Zen Den service notes, Fresha

Treatments that work best as a course

A few treatments are designed to build. Zen Den's own guidance on how many sessions, and how often:

Ace of Face Facial Lifting

For best results, a course of six weekly sessions is recommended, followed by monthly maintenance.

G8 Body Sculpt

A single session helps with muscle tension. A course of eight weekly sessions is recommended for lasting sculpting and contouring results.

Source: Zen Den service notes, Fresha
