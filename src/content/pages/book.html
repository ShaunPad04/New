Book a patch test if it’s your first

Any tinting, perming or lifting treatment needs a free fifteen-minute patch test at least 24 hours beforehand — 48 hours for HD Brow Sculpt. Add it to the same booking. Without it the appointment can’t go ahead, and the time may still be charged.
