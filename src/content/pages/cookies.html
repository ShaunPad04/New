The short version

This site sets nothing on your device for advertising, and nothing that follows you to other websites.

Statistics are off until you say yes. The only thing stored without asking is the map, which comes from Google and brings Google's own cookies with it.

Analytics, only if you agree

We would like to know which treatments people read about and whether anyone gets lost, so we can improve the site. That is all analytics is used for here.

If you agree, Google Analytics is loaded and your IP address is shortened before it is recorded, so the statistics cannot be traced back to you. If you refuse, or simply ignore the banner, nothing is loaded and no analytics cookie is set.

Your answer is remembered in your browser's own storage rather than in a cookie, and it never leaves your device.

The map

The map showing the salon is supplied by Google and loads with the page. Your browser fetches it directly from Google, which means Google receives your IP address and may set its own cookies before you have chosen anything here.

We would rather that were not the case. To avoid it, block third-party cookies in your browser, or use the written address and directions link instead of the map.

Fresha and social links

Some photographs of the salon are served from Fresha, so those pages contact Fresha's servers. Following any booking button, or our Facebook and Instagram links, takes you to those companies, where their own cookie policies apply.

Changing your mind

You can withdraw or give consent at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings, which resets the banner so you are asked again.

You can also block or delete cookies entirely in your browser. Nothing on this site needs cookies in order to work — every page, price and opening time is readable with them switched off.
