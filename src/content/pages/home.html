Since the beginning

Zen Den is a small centre on Fieldhouse Road, run by Natalie with Kayleigh and Alisia. It was built as a sanctuary for people who value relaxation, rejuvenation and thoughtful self-care.

More than 25 years of experience sits behind it, along with advanced knowledge in counselling studies and a holistic approach to supporting how you feel — not only how you look.
